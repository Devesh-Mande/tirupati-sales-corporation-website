About Us

Tirupati Sales Corporation is a leading supplier of industrial, mechanical, hand tools and maintenance items based in Cidco, Chh. Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), Maharashtra.

We are authorized dealers for some of India's most reputed industrial brands — Akar, Totem, Bradma, Fenner, Speb7, Sterling, SVS, Laser Technologies and Eastman — ensuring our customers always receive genuine, high-quality products.

Whether you are a factory engineer, a maintenance contractor, or a small workshop owner — we have the right tools for your job. Our knowledgeable team helps you find the exact product that fits your technical requirement and budget.
